# Rwad Room — How Everything Works

### What is Rwad Room?
Rwad Room is a club for people who run businesses in Bahrain. You join, and you get access to helpful stuff — free guides, discounts from partner companies, and mentors who can help you figure things out.

---

### I'm a **Startup Founder**. What do I do?

1. **Sign up** — Go to the website, pick "Startup Founder", fill in your name and email
2. **Check your email** — We send you a link. Click it. That proves it's really you
3. **Wait a bit** — Someone from our team checks your application. You'll see a nice screen that says "hang tight"
4. **Fill out your profile** — Once approved, add your company name, what you do, and upload a logo
5. **Pay your membership** — One payment per year. This unlocks everything
6. **Read the good stuff** — Guides, templates, checklists — all yours to download
7. **Get discounts** — Partner companies give special deals only to members
8. **Talk to a mentor** — Pick someone smart, tell us why you want to meet them, and we set it up
9. **Ask for introductions** — Want to meet a partner company? We'll connect you
10. **Check your requests** — See if your intro or mentor meeting is confirmed yet

---

### I'm a **Mentor**. What do I do?

1. **Sign up** — Pick "Mentor" when you register
2. **Verify + wait** — Same email check, same approval wait
3. **Build your profile** — Write your bio, list what you're good at, add your LinkedIn and a photo
4. **That's it** — Startups will find you in the directory and request sessions through us

---

### I'm a **Partner Company**. What do I do?

1. **You don't sign up yourself** — An admin sends you an invite by email
2. **Set your password** — Click the link in the email and pick a password
3. **Add your company info** — Describe what you do, what discount you're offering, and upload your logo
4. **Members see you** — Your company shows up in the partner list with your discount

---

### I'm an **Admin**. What can I do?

1. **Approve people** — New users show up as "pending". You say yes or no
2. **Fix subscriptions** — If someone's payment is stuck or you want to give a free trial, you can turn their membership on or off
3. **Add resources** — Upload PDFs, spreadsheets, or paste links. Pick who can see them (everyone or members only)
4. **Organize resources** — Create categories like "Business Plans" or "Legal Templates"
5. **Handle introductions** — When a startup asks to meet a mentor or partner, you see the request and coordinate it
6. **Invite partners** — Type in a company's email, and they get an invite to join
7. **Make other admins** — Promote someone if you need help managing things

---

### I'm just visiting. What can I see?

1. **The homepage** — What Rwad Room is and why it exists
2. **Partner list** — You can see the companies, but the discounts are blurred out (join to see them)
3. **Mentor list** — You can see who the mentors are, but you can't book anything yet
4. **Resource preview** — You can see what's available, but the good stuff is locked for members
